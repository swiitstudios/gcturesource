About GCTU Resource

Built by a GCTU student, for GCTU students.

Why this exists

Finding lecture slides at GCTU means chasing lecturers and seniors, and scrolling through WhatsApp groups full of PDFs just to find one lesson note. GCTU Resource puts slides, past questions and study materials in one place, from Level 100 to Level 400.

The goal is simple: spend less time hunting, more time studying.
